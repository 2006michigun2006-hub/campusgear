# Desk interview prompts (S1-01, E-01)

- **Owner:** Product Owner. **Support:** Scrum Master (notes).
- **Format:** a 20-minute call, or the written question set below sent by email.
- **Who:** one person from the Library desk and one from the IT help desk. Their names are not recorded in the repository, only their role and the date.

**Decision owner for every answer:** the desk lead. If an answer needs someone else (asset owner, IT security), write that down as "TBD, owner: …".

Record each answer in `docs/rule-log.md`: change the rule's status to Confirmed and add the date. If there is no answer, leave the rule Provisional. Never mark a rule as approved because nobody objected.

## Opening and handoff
1. On which days and hours does your desk hand out and take back equipment? Is there a lunch break?
2. Is the desk staffed during exam weeks and holidays?
3. How long does one handoff take? Could two students arrive at the same time?
4. Should pickup be possible at any full hour, or only at set times (for example 10:00, 12:00, 14:00)?

## Loan length and booking window
5. What is the longest loan you would allow for a laptop? For a projector?
6. How far ahead should students be able to book? Is 1 to 14 days right?
7. Should a student be able to hold more than one item at once?

## No-shows, late returns, faults
8. If a student does not come, how long do you wait before giving the item to someone else?
9. What do you do today when an item comes back late? (The pilot will not charge fees or send messages.)
10. When an item breaks, who decides it is out of service? Who decides when it is back?
11. If a broken item has bookings, who tells the students?

## Inventory and records
12. Who owns the list of loanable equipment? Can we see the fields it has (no real serial numbers needed)?
13. What do you check at checkout and return (charger, cables, condition)?
14. Do you need a paper or signed record today? Must the pilot keep one?

## Pilot permission and privacy
15. Would you take part in a 15-minute observed test of the staff screens (`staff-task-sheet.md`)?
16. Who must approve a pilot with real students? Who decides how long loan history is kept?
